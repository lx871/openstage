# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind is not tolerated.
- Violations may be reported to the maintainers and will be handled confidentially.

Enforcement decisions are final and will be communicated transparently where appropriate.
